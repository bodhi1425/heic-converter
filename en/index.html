FAQ

FAQ

What is HEIC and why won't my photos open?
HEIC/HEIF is iPhone's default photo format since iOS 11 — small and high quality, but many Android phones, Windows PCs and websites don't support it, so it won't open. Convert to JPG or PNG and any device can view it.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser (libheif WebAssembly); photos are never uploaded.
Can I batch-convert multiple files?
Yes. Drop several at once; download each, or download all as a single ZIP.
JPG or PNG — which should I pick?
JPG for photos (smaller, great for sharing); PNG for transparency/lossless. For most iPhone photos, JPG is fine.
